DEHA ONE is built with security at its core. Your data stays on your own infrastructure, your workspace is fully isolated, your access is controlled, and your credentials are encrypted. This page is an overview of how DEHA ONE keeps your account safe.

<Info>
  **Your data. Your cloud. Your rules.** DEHA ONE is built around data sovereignty -- the data side runs on your own infrastructure and the platform keeps only the operational layer. See [Bring Your Own Data](/data/versioning-and-byod).
</Info>

---

## Your workspace is fully isolated

Every organization on DEHA ONE operates in its own isolated workspace. Isolation is enforced at every layer -- your data, your knowledge, your credentials, your configuration, your usage, and your activity history are all kept separate. **Cross-workspace access is structurally impossible**, so one customer can never see another's data, results, or settings.

---

## How you sign in

DEHA ONE uses **single sign-on (SSO)** for dashboard access, built on open identity standards (OIDC).

- Sign in through your company's identity provider
- Sessions are verified on every request and expire automatically after a period of inactivity
- New team members can be auto-provisioned on first sign-in (configurable per role)

For programmatic access (your apps, CI/CD, integrations), use API keys instead -- see [API Keys](/security/api-keys).

---

## Team access control

Every team member is assigned a role that determines what they can see and do. DEHA ONE provides five built-in roles, from full administrative access to read-only viewing.

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and team management |
| **Admin** | Manage configurations, deploy changes, full operational access |
| **Supervisor** | Edit configurations (cannot deploy to production) |
| **Agent** | Handle conversations and human review tasks |
| **Viewer** | View dashboards and reports (read-only) |

See [Roles and Permissions](/security/roles-and-permissions) for the full breakdown.

## API keys for programmatic access

When you need to connect external systems to DEHA ONE -- automation tools, custom applications, or backend services -- create API keys from the dashboard. Three scopes are supported:

| Scope | What it can access |
|---|---|
| **Account key** | Every agent and pipeline in your workspace |
| **Per-agent key** | Only one specific agent |
| **Per-pipeline key** | Only one specific pipeline |

Every key:

- Is securely hashed at rest (the raw key is shown only once, on creation)
- Honors per-key rate limits
- Supports a per-key IP allow-list
- Can be rotated or revoked instantly
- Is recorded in an audit log on every use

See [API Keys](/security/api-keys) for setup instructions.

---

## IP allow-lists

Your workspace and each individual agent or pipeline can have an IP allow-list, set as exact addresses or ranges. Requests from any other address are rejected before they reach your setup.

- Account-level allow-lists apply to everything in your workspace
- Per-agent and per-pipeline allow-lists scope further
- Manage them from the dashboard or via the API

---

## How we protect your credentials

All sensitive values -- API tokens, database passwords, OAuth credentials, certificates -- are stored in an encrypted vault. Your agents and integrations reference these secrets by name, so the actual values never appear in your configuration.

- Strong encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Secrets are resolved only at the moment they're needed, and are never logged
- Every vault access is recorded in an audit log

See [BYOK & Vault](/security/byok-and-vault) for the full model, including bringing your own AI provider keys.

---

## Encryption

All communication with DEHA ONE is encrypted:

- **In transit**: all traffic uses HTTPS (TLS)
- **At rest**: sensitive data and secrets are encrypted in storage

---

## Auditable by default

Every action, result, and AI decision is recorded and traceable, so you can answer any auditor and adopt AI with confidence. Webhooks from external channels are signature-verified to ensure messages are authentic and untampered.
