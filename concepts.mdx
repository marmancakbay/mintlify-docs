DEHA ONE is built around one simple idea: **you set the intent, and the platform carries it all the way to a finished outcome.** Everything in between -- understanding your data, planning the work, acting on it, and presenting the result -- happens as a single continuous flow.

We give each stage of that flow a name. Together they describe how DEHA ONE turns a sentence into a working system.

<Info>
  You don't need to learn these names to use the platform. They're simply the vocabulary we use across the product and these docs, so you always know which part of the flow you're looking at.
</Info>

---

## One flow, from intent to outcome

<Steps>
  <Step title="İRADE — Your will" icon="crown">
    **You.** You set the intent in plain language, and the finished result comes back to you. The whole flow starts and ends here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="İMKAN — Your potential" icon="database">
    **Your raw data,** untouched and on your own infrastructure. This is the raw material the platform works from -- and it never leaves your control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="BELLEK — Memory" icon="brain">
    **What the platform remembers.** Your past context, decisions, and knowledge, so the platform gets better for you over time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="İDRAK — Understanding" icon="diagram-project">
    **A living map of all your data and what every piece of it means.** İDRAK connects your tables, files, and documents into one picture you can explore and ask questions of.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DEHA — The mind" icon="sparkles">
    **The intelligence that understands, plans, and runs everything.** Describe what you need, and DEHA designs it, deploys it, and keeps it healthy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="KURUL — The council" icon="users">
    **The decision-making agents that act on your behalf.** A team of specialist agents that plan, use real tools, and hand work off to one another -- with you in control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="EYLEM — Action" icon="gears">
    **The work itself,** executed reliably and repeatably as automations and pipelines -- on a schedule, on an event, or on demand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="SUNU — Presentation" icon="file-lines">
    **The result, presented back to you** as a report, a dashboard, or a polished document -- ready to share.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Read it as a loop: **İRADE → İMKAN → BELLEK → İDRAK → DEHA → KURUL → EYLEM → SUNU → İRADE.** It begins with your intent and ends with a result in your hands.
</Tip>

## Why it's one platform, not many tools

Most teams stitch together a data tool, an analytics tool, an automation tool, an agent framework, and a reporting tool -- and then spend their time keeping the glue from breaking.

DEHA ONE is **one flow**. Your data feeds understanding, understanding feeds the agents, the agents drive the work, and the work produces a result you can share -- all in one place, all traceable end to end, all driven by what you ask for in plain language.
