The most valuable questions usually can't be answered by one table or one document alone. *"Which of our top customers have open complaints, and what were they about?"* spans your orders, your support records, and your documents all at once.

İDRAK answers exactly these questions. Ask in plain language, and it draws the answer from across **all** of your connected data, structured records, documents, and the relationships İDRAK has mapped between them, and shows you where every part of the answer came from.

---

## How it works for you

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask in plain language">
    Type your question the way you'd ask a colleague. No query language, no knowing which table or file holds the answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="İDRAK pulls from everywhere at once">
    It looks across your data map, your structured records, and your documents together, combining what it finds rather than searching just one place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get a cited answer">
    The answer comes back in plain language, with citations pointing to the exact records and documents it was built from, so you can trust it and verify it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why it's more than search

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="It understands connections" icon="share-nodes">
    Because İDRAK knows how your data relates, it can follow a thread, from a customer, to their orders, to a contract that mentions them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="It blends data and documents" icon="layer-group">
    Numbers from your databases and context from your documents come together in a single answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Every answer is grounded" icon="quote-right">
    Answers cite their sources. Nothing is made up, if it's in the answer, you can see where it came from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Available to your agents" icon="robot">
    The same capability your team uses is available to your [agents](/agents/overview) and [automations](/automations/overview), so they can reason over your data too.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where you can ask

- **From the dashboard**, explore your data map and ask questions directly.
- **Through DEHA**, your [mastermind](/deha/overview) can answer questions about your data as part of a conversation.
- **Inside automations**, make "ask the data map" a step in a [pipeline](/automations/overview), so answers flow into reports, alerts, or decisions.
- **Through your agents**, give an [agent](/agents/overview) the ability to consult your data map while it works.

<Tip>
  Pair this with [Reports & Presentations](/viz/overview) to turn a question into a scheduled, shareable document, e.g. a weekly brief that answers the same set of questions every Monday.
</Tip>
