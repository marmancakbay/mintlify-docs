The value of a data map is in the connections. İDRAK finds those connections **for** you: it looks at your data, proposes how things relate, and lets you confirm the ones that matter -- so you get an accurate map without drawing it by hand.

---

## Two kinds of discovery

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Connections between tables" icon="table-cells">
    İDRAK detects when records in one table refer to records in another -- like orders that belong to customers, or invoices tied to accounts -- by spotting where the same real-world keys appear across your data.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connections between documents" icon="file-lines">
    İDRAK detects when documents are about the same things -- related contracts, similar reports, files that cover the same topic -- and links them together.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Together, these turn a pile of separate sources into one connected picture.

---

## You stay in control

İDRAK proposes; you decide. Discovery is **semi-automatic** by design: the platform does the heavy lifting of finding candidate relationships, and you review the ones that matter before they become part of your official map.

<Steps>
  <Step title="İDRAK proposes connections">
    It surfaces the relationships it found, ranked by how confident it is, so the strongest candidates are front and center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You review and confirm">
    Approve the connections that are right and dismiss the ones that aren't, from a simple review inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your decisions are remembered">
    Confirmed connections become a permanent part of your map. When İDRAK re-discovers later, it refreshes machine-found links but **never overwrites the ones you confirmed.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  İDRAK is careful about false connections. When two things only *look* related by coincidence, it holds back rather than cluttering your map -- and it leans on your confirmation for anything it isn't sure about.
</Info>

---

## No setup, no maintenance

- **No schema modeling.** You don't define relationships up front -- İDRAK proposes them from your actual data.
- **No AI required for discovery.** Finding and refreshing connections is fast, predictable, and repeatable.
- **Stays current on its own.** As your data grows, İDRAK re-checks for new connections on a schedule and brings the fresh ones to you.
