Most organizations don't have a data problem -- they have a *meaning* problem. The data exists, but it's scattered across databases, spreadsheets, and documents, and nobody has the full picture of how it all connects.

**İDRAK is DEHA ONE's living map of all your data and what it means.** Point it at your sources and it discovers your tables, files, and documents, works out how they relate to one another, and assembles them into one picture you can explore visually and ask questions of in plain language.

<Info>
  İDRAK holds the **meaning** of your data -- the entities, the relationships, and pointers back to where each thing lives. Your actual business data stays in your own sources. İDRAK never copies or keeps it. (See [Bring Your Own Data](/data/versioning-and-byod).)
</Info>

## From raw sources to a living map

<Steps>
  <Step title="Point it at your data">
    Connect a database, a folder of files, or a set of documents. You can also just ask DEHA: *"Discover everything under this folder and build a map of it."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="It discovers and maps">
    İDRAK profiles each source, identifies the real-world entities inside it (customers, orders, products, contracts...), and lays them out as a map -- no schema design required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It connects the dots">
    İDRAK proposes how things relate -- which table links to which, and which documents are about the same things -- and surfaces the important ones for you to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You explore and ask">
    Browse the map to understand your data at a glance, or just ask questions in plain language and get cited answers drawn from across everything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How İDRAK fits the rest of the platform

İDRAK is the **understanding** layer of the [DEHA ONE model](/concepts). It sits between your raw data and the intelligence that acts on it:

- Your [agents](/agents/overview) and [DEHA](/deha/overview) use İDRAK to understand your data before they act on it.
- It works hand in hand with your [knowledge base](/knowledge-base/overview): structured data and documents become one connected picture.
- It builds on your [data connections](/data/connecting-data), so anything you connect can become part of the map.

<Tip>
  İDRAK is built to run **without any AI calls** for discovery and mapping -- it's fast, repeatable, and predictable. AI is used only when *you* ask a question in plain language.
</Tip>
