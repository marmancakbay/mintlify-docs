Channels are how DEHA ONE reaches people: your agents and automations can **send results, alerts, and reports** to a messaging platform, and **accept input** back from it. It's an input/output layer, not the product, so the same setup works no matter which channel is connected.

<Info>
  Channels are optional. Most of DEHA ONE runs without them, your data, agents, automations, and reports work entirely on their own. Add a channel only when you want results delivered to, or input collected from, a messaging platform.
</Info>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="WhatsApp" icon="whatsapp" href="/channels/whatsapp">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Slack" icon="slack" href="/channels/slack">

Email and Telegram are also supported and can be enabled from the dashboard. Need another platform (Teams, SMS, or a custom webhook)? Reach out to your account team.

---

## How it fits

- **One setup, every channel.** Whatever you build works across any connected channel without changes.
- **Delivery is reliable.** Outbound messages are sent with automatic retries and failure recovery.
- **Approvals work everywhere.** If an automation pauses for [human review](/automations/human-review), your team handles it from the dashboard, regardless of channel.
- **Usage is tracked per channel**, so you can see which ones are active.

<Tip>
  The most common use is **output**: have a [pipeline](/automations/overview) publish a daily report or an alert to Slack or WhatsApp on a schedule. See the `publish` building block in [Automations](/automations/step-types).
</Tip>

## Setting up a channel

1. Go to **Settings → Channels** in your dashboard
2. Enable the channel you want
3. Follow its setup steps (linking your WhatsApp Business account, Slack workspace, etc.)

<Info>
  All credentials are stored securely in your encrypted vault and never exposed.
</Info>
