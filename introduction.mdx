## Why DEHA ONE

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Build with natural language">
    DEHA, your built-in AI mastermind, lets you describe what you want in plain language. It builds the setup, validates it, deploys it, runs tests, and auto-heals if something fails. No code required for the vast majority of use cases.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Understands all your data">
    İDRAK assembles your tables, files, and documents into one living map and works out how they connect, so you (and your agents) can ask questions across everything at once and get answers with their sources shown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Works with any AI model">
    Use leading AI providers or your own self-hosted models. Pick a model per agent, swap models without rebuilding, or use simple aliases like `fast`, `smart`, or `cheap` and let the platform route.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bring Your Own Data (BYOD)">
    Your business data stays on your own infrastructure. DEHA ONE processes data but does not own it, results sink back to your own databases and storage. The platform keeps only the operational layer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bring Your Own Keys (BYOK)">
    Use your own AI provider credentials. Embeddings, completions, and tools run on your accounts, with your rate limits and your billing relationship.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Secure and auditable by default">
    Single sign-on, fine-grained roles, per-workspace isolation, an encrypted vault for credentials, and a complete, auditable activity trail, so you can adopt AI with confidence and answer any auditor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transparent, credit-based pricing">
    Pay only for what you use. Monitor consumption in real time, set quota alerts, and control spending with configurable overage actions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
